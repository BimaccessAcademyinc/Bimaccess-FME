# Gabarits de composition

Compositions qui ont donné une planche juste du premier coup sur FME001-M01-L01. Reprendre le texte, changer les objets ; positions en % de l'image. Chaque gabarit dit aussi quels éléments demander à la vision.

## Territoire et parties (S01)

> A territory map. A large irregular region outline drawn as a dash-dot ink boundary line, spanning from 4% to 96% of the width and from 22% to 79% of the height; inside, a winding blue river and a few faint brown contour lines. Near the top center (x 40-56%, y 24-36%): a small pale card containing two small standing human silhouettes side by side. On the left (x 8-26%, y 40-54%): a pale card with one standing silhouette and three small conifer tree symbols. On the right (x 72-90%, y 40-54%): a pale card with one standing silhouette and a small official building pictogram with columns. From the right edge of the left card, a thick blue horizontal line goes right and stops at about 48% of the width, ending on a small gold square marker with pale yellow fill.

Vision : le territoire, chaque carte, la liaison, le manque.

## Frise ouverte (S02)

> A horizontal timeline across the sheet at 57% of the height, from 4% to 76% of the width: a thick ink line ending on the right with an open solid arrowhead. Two milestones: a tall vertical ink tick with a round dot at 34% and at 62% of the width. […] Keep empty: the area below the timeline (y 60-80%) everywhere.

Vision : la frise, chaque jalon, les objets au-dessus. Pour qu'un jalon paraisse seul, le dessiner détaché de la ligne.

## Légende de carte (S03)

> On the left (x 5-46%, y 22-80%): a tall pale legend cartouche with a thin double rule near its top, leaving an empty header band. Inside, along its left edge, three small symbol boxes stacked vertically […]. To the right of each symbol, an empty line.

Vision : la légende, chaque case de symbole ; les lignes de texte en `zones`.

## Feuille à rubriques (S04)

> On the right (x 40-72%, y 22-80%): a large pale sheet with a thin double rule at its top (empty header band), divided into three stacked sections separated by thin rules, each with an empty header line at its top: section 1 […]; section 2 […]; section 3 […].

Vision : la feuille, chaque rubrique, et chaque objet qui voyagera (« only that outline »).

## Chaîne de traitement (S05)

> A left-to-right chain at mid height (all elements between y 28% and 58%). Left: a pale format sheet with a light blue water pattern; inside it a small entity card. A blue route with arrowhead. Center: a pale block with a double outline, three small tool pictograms at its top and the same small entity card at its bottom. A red route with arrowhead. Right: a format sheet with a green hatching; inside it the same small entity card, its polygon now filled with the green hatching.

Une copie de l'objet dessinée à chaque étape : le plan la fait voyager (`deplace`).

## Étoile autour d'un centre (S06)

> At the center: a geodesic point symbol surrounded by three concentric brown contour rings. Around it, a ring of seven small pale format sheets, each with a different map pattern inside, at these centers: (14%, 50%) blue water; (24%, 30%) cadastre parcels; (46%, 25%) building footprints; (68%, 30%) brown contour lines; (76%, 50%) roads; (64%, 72%) green vegetation; (28%, 72%) aerial image raster. Blue routes from the three left sheets to the center; red routes from the center to the four others.

Vision : le centre, l'anneau des feuilles, les routes bleues, les routes rouges ; `couleurs` pour les routes.

## Pont : deux manques (S07)
